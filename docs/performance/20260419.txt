## Notes per workload

fib(28)
    5 runs, median ≈54 ms JIT-on, 50 ms JIT-off.  Run-to-run spread
    ≤2 ms either way.  JIT stats show activations=0 / compiled=0 for
    this bench because `PHARO_JIT_DEFER=4s` (headless default) fires
    AFTER fib completes in 54 ms.  The "JIT on" column here is
    effectively the interpreter — JIT never engages.

sieve x3
    Integer>>benchmark called three times.  Identical 4 ms result JIT
    on and off, same reason as fib: defer masks JIT.

tinyBenchmarks
    JIT actually engages here — 5.6 s run time is well past the 4 s
    defer window.  27 M IC hits, 113 IC misses (99.9996 % hit), 61
    J2J patches, 197 k OSR entries.  JIT wall time 5.6 s vs
    interpreter 7.1 s → JIT is 21 % faster on this workload.

    This is the first time tinyBenchmarks completes cleanly under
    JIT — the stored 2026-04-17 result was a hang.  Fixed somewhere
    between then and today; likely the scheduler fix (a2b99f7)
    since the earlier hang signature looked scheduler-related.


## What changed since 2026-04-17

Correctness wins:
- tinyBenchmarks JIT: hang → 5.6 s clean.
- fib(28) JIT: "timeout" → 54 ms clean.

Peak-speed remains the same:
- Arith-bound micro-benches (fib, sieve) still dominated by defer
  masking JIT entirely, so effective peak is interpreter-only.
- The 04-17 root-cause analysis (460-byte stencils busting M1's
  128-byte i-cache line, 200+ indirect-branch targets saturating
  the predictor) is unchanged — those are architectural.


## Where we stand vs Cog

fib(28) JIT (effectively interp): 27× slower.
sieve x3:                          1.3× slower.

fib is recursion-heavy / send-bound, where Cog's threaded-code
dispatch dominates.  sieve is arith-heavy, where our interpreter's
inline primitive fast-paths close the gap.

Stored AWFY geomean was 30× (richer workload; Richards/Queens/Towers
are the worst offenders).  Fib matches that order.  Without AWFY
classes pre-loaded in the Pharo 13 image, we can't rerun the full
AWFY matrix today.


## AWFY not run

The stock Pharo 13 image used here has no AWFY classes loaded
(Richards, DeltaBlue, Mandelbrot, etc.).  The stored 2026-04-11 AWFY
numbers came from an image with AWFY pre-installed; reproducing that
comparison requires a fresh image with AWFY loaded.  fib + sieve +
tinyBenchmarks are the signal available from a stock Pharo 13 image.
