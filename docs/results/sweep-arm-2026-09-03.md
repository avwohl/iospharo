## Why this run exists

The 2026-09-02 sweep lost 39 classes when batch 1901-1950 aborted on a Context
storm (defect #23).  The storm is now bounded: on its five-class repro batch
under held load the guard takes aborts from 7 of 10 to 0 of 10, firing in 7 of
those runs.  This sweep is the question that matters -- does the whole suite
still run clean with a guard that terminates processes?
