Record

  • State before: stock Cog 42 of 42 pass. Our VM: 34 pass, 8 skip. The 8 tests of NewFileTest start with NewFile primitiveIsAvailable ifFalse: [ ^ self skip ].
  • Cause: a defect of our VM. The package calls the plugin NewFilePlugin of the stock VM, and our VM did not have the plugin.
  • Repair: src/vm/plugins/NewFilePlugin.cpp (20 primitives, the POSIX side). The package needs no change.
  • Result: 42 of 42 pass on the two VMs.
  • The tests of the package reach 14 of the 20 primitives. scripts/cogdiff/newfile_plugin_checks.st has 36 more checks (directories, closed handles, buffers that are not ByteArrays, wrong arguments). Our VM and stock Cog give the same answer for 33 of them. The 3 others are defects of the stock plugin:
    • A read of 3 bytes into a ByteArray of 2 bytes answers 3: the stock plugin writes past the buffer. Our VM fails the primitive.
    • A read into an Array answers 2: the stock plugin writes bytes into the pointer slots of the Array. Our VM fails the primitive.
    • A read into a read-only ByteArray answers 2. Our VM fails the primitive.
  • scripts/cogdiff/newfile_plugin_ours_checks.st is for our VM only. Stock Cog stopped with Abort trap: 6 in primitiveClose: when the first form of the script had the check that gives a copy of the handle of a closed file to the close primitive (the stock plugin keeps a C pointer in the handle). Our VM keeps an id that is never used again, and fails the primitive.
  • Found in the package, not repaired, and no test shows the defect: NewFile>>close does not release the memory views of the file, and sets the handle to null. A later unmap of a view then gives a null handle to the primitive, and the mapping stays until the VM exits. The stock VM has the same result.
